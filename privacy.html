Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026

Short version

No accounts, no tracking, no ads, no email list. Sixty Bucks keeps your score in your own browser and never sends it anywhere. Diva Bingo asks for a display name so the room knows who you are. That is the whole story.

What this site does not do

asiandiva.games has no analytics, no advertising, and no way for you to make an account. Specifically, there is:

  • No sign-up, login, or password
  • No analytics or visitor tracking of any kind
  • No advertising and no advertising networks
  • No cookies
  • No newsletter, mailing list, or contact form
  • No selling or sharing of your information with anyone

The two games each need a little something to work, and both are described below.

Diva Bingo

Diva Bingo is played in a live room, so it needs a game server. When you join a room, your browser sends the following to that server:

  • The display name you type in, so the host and the other players can see who is in the room and who called the bingo
  • The room code you were given
  • How many cards you chose, and which squares you mark as the game runs, so the server can check whether a bingo is real

That is the whole list. There is no account attached to it, it is not linked to you as a person, and it is not used for anything outside of the game you are playing. Your display name is visible to everyone else in that room, so if you would rather not be identifiable, pick a nickname.

Sixty Bucks

Sixty Bucks does not use a server at all. Your guesses are checked right there in your browser.

So that you can only play each day's set once, the game saves the date, your score, and your six results in your browser's local storage on your own device. That information never leaves your device, is never sent to me or anyone else, and is not linked to you. Clearing your browser data erases it and lets you play the day over.

The share button hands your result text to your own device's copy or share function. Where it goes from there is up to you and whichever app you pick.

Fonts and cover art

Two things on this site are loaded from other companies' servers, which means your browser contacts them directly and they receive your IP address and basic browser information as part of that request. I don't receive any of that and I don't use it.

  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces used by both games. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Steam's image servers supply the game cover art in Sixty Bucks. This site is not affiliated with Valve. See Valve's privacy policy.

Web hosting

This site and its bingo server are hosted by Render, and the code behind it is stored on GitHub. Like nearly every web host, Render may automatically record standard technical information such as IP addresses and browser type in their server logs, for security and for keeping the site online. That happens at the hosting level and is not something I request, control, or have access to. Their privacy policies are on their own websites: Render and GitHub.

Links to other sites

This site links out to other places, including my Twitch channel. Once you follow a link, you are on someone else's site and their privacy policy applies, not this one. I'd recommend giving those a read if you're curious what they do.

Children

The games here are meant for a general audience. This site does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The only thing it ever asks anyone for is a display name to play bingo with, and a nickname works perfectly well for that.

Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you the right to see, correct, or delete the personal information a site holds about you. The only thing this site ever receives is the display name you type into a bingo room. Your Sixty Bucks scores are yours already, sitting in your own browser, and you can clear them yourself at any time. If you have a question about any of this, or you want it confirmed in writing, email me at the address below and I'll sort it out.

Changes to this policy

If this site ever starts collecting anything new, for example if I add analytics or a leaderboard that remembers you between games, I'll update this page and change the date at the top before that goes live.
